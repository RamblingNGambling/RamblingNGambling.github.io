How to read it

The five price fields are five ways of writing the same price. Fill in whichever one you have and read off the rest.

Legs on the parlay tab take any of the five forms as well, one per leg.

Fees are charged on top of the bet amount and do not come back, so they come out of To win and make your true price worse. Leave the box empty if the book takes none.

When the legs do not multiply to the payout

On a combo ticket the leg multipliers printed on the card often do not multiply to the payout the book has committed to. They are display values, rounded or refreshed, and not always the prices the ticket was built from. The cost and the payout are the two numbers the book is bound by, so their ratio is the real price of the ticket. Divide the payout by the cost and put that in the multiplier field.
