What the numbers mean

Units, win rate, and how to check the plays.

The numbers

A unit is one standard bet. Everything here is in units, so the record reads the same whether you bet $5 or $500. A 2u play is a double-size bet.

You can win under half your bets and still make money, because the payouts differ. At −110 you risk 110 to win 100, so you need 52.4% to break even. At +150 you risk 100 to win 150, so 40% is enough.

ROI is units won over units risked. A push returns the stake. Voids and unsettled plays don't count.
