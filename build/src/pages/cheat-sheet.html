Percent to American odds

Every percentage from 1 to 99, whole or in halves, with the price that pays fairly for it. Use it to read a Kalshi, Novig or Polymarket price as a sportsbook line.

Quick look-up

How to read it

Find your chance in the left column of a block. The price beside it is the one that breaks even at that chance. A price better than that one is a bet with value.

Under 50% the price is a plus number. You risk one unit to win more than one. Over 50% the price is a minus number. You risk more than one unit to win one. At 50% the price is +100, an even bet.

These prices carry no vig. A real book prices both sides so that the two chances add up to more than 100%. Use the devig tool to strip that out first, then come back here.
