How to read it

Set the buy-in, then add everyone at the table. Each player starts on one buy-in. Press + for each rebuy. For an odd amount, such as a half buy-in, type the total into the In column.

At the end, type what each player cashed out. Type 0 for a player who lost it all.

When every cash-out is in and they add up to the buy-ins, the payments show here. If they do not add up, the page says by how much. Recount, or split the difference.

How the payments are worked out

Each player’s result is what they cashed out less what they put in. The player who is down the most pays the player who is up the most, as much as one of them needs. That repeats until everyone is square. A table of eight settles in seven payments or fewer.

Nobody pays the host and waits to be paid back. Every payment goes straight from a loser to a winner.

When the count is off

Chips go missing. A rebuy goes unrecorded. A stack is counted wrong. Then the cash-outs do not add up to the buy-ins, and there is no way to pay everyone exactly.

Recount first. If the gap is still there, Split shares it across the table by the size of each result. A player up $200 takes twice the share of a player down $100. A player who broke even takes none.

When chips are missing, the gap is handed back: winners collect a little more and losers pay a little less. When the count comes out over, it works the other way.

Say $10 is missing, and three players are up $190, down $100 and down $100. The results come to $390 between them, so the winner takes 190/390 of the $10 and each loser 100/390. The winner collects $194.87, and the losers pay $97.43 and $97.44. The odd cent has to land somewhere.

Your game stays on your device

The table is kept in this browser only, so a reload in the middle of a game loses nothing. New game clears it. Copy link puts the whole table into the address, so anyone you send it to opens the same numbers.
